Terms of Trade (TOT)
1. Supply & Invoicing

a) The Vendor shall supply the Products to the Company at its warehouse and/or any other place as instructed by the Company by way of raising Purchase Orders from time to time.

b) The Vendor shall raise a tax invoice to the Company while supplying the Products. The Vendor shall fix the Maximum Retail Price (MRP) of the Products (wherever applicable) and send the list of MRP of the Products to the Company.

2. Taxes/GST Obligations

a) The Vendor shall issue invoice to the Company in compliance with the provisions of GST Act, 2017, Rules and Directions.

b) The Vendor shall file the GSTR1 and GSTR3B and other GST returns on or before the due date.

c) The Vendor shall be liable to compensate any loss to the Company attributable to Vendor's failure in compliance of GST laws.

d) Any indirect tax leviable by the state/central government other than GST and any other charges/fees shall be borne by the Vendor.

e) The Vendor shall issue/generate e-way bills and e-invoices in accordance with the GST Act, Rules and Directions made thereunder or by any applicable laws.

f) In case of return of Products by the Company then the Company shall issue a Delivery chalan/debit note to the Vendor. Subsequently the Vendor agrees to issue a credit note within the time period prescribed under the applicable GST or any other Laws.

g) If the products are returned after the time period prescribed under GST law or any other law, a B2B invoice will be issued as per the applicable law.

h) B2B invoice can be issued by the Company, even during the time period prescribed under GST laws, if the Vendor raises a request for the same.

i) In the event a B2B invoice is issued by the Company as par sub-clauses (g) and (h) above for the return of goods, the Vendor shall accept the B2B invoice and shall not report credit note in GST returns for such transactions.

j) The Company shall have the right to recover any loss including loss of ITC (Input Tax Credit) claim incurred by the Company on account of Vendor's failure to report tax documents such as invoice/ debit note/ credit note in their GST returns or in any other documents/ as required by any applicable Laws or rules.

k) The Company shall have the right to deduct or withhold any taxes, which it is obligated to withhold from any amount due to the Vendor, and such payment made by withholding taxes/deduction will be treated as full and final settlement of payments towards the Vendor.

3. Compliance & Regulations

a) All products supplied by the Vendor are packed and labelled in accordance with the provisions of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009, the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodity Rules), 2011 and all other rules, regulations and directions issued by the authorities from time to time applicable in respect of packaging and labelling of products in India.

b) All products supplied by the Vendor shall be in compliance with the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 and the rules made thereunder containing BIS/ISI marks. The Vendor shall provide the Bureau of Indian Standards certificates to the Company for the product supplied.

c) All food products manufactured, supplied by the Vendor shall be in compliance with Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006/Rules/Directions issued by the authorities from time to time. The Vendor shall provide the FSSAI License to the Company.

d) The Vendor hereby certifies that food/foods supplied to the Company are warranted to be the nature and quality which they are purported to be.

e) The Vendor shall be solely responsible for illness, diseases, death or injury caused due to usage/consumption of products supplied by the Vendor.

f) If any discrepancy is found in the supplied products during inspection of concerned government department, Vendor alone will be responsible for penalties and other Charges, if any, levied by the department for any statutory default.

4. Intellectual Property

a) The Company is the sole owner of its trademarks, service marks, trade names, trade secrets, corporate slogans, corporate logos, product designations (collectively the "Marks") and its goodwill.

b) The Vendor agrees that they shall have no right to use the intellectual Property rights of the Company in any mode or manner without the prior written consent of the Company.

5. Damaged/Defective Products and Return Policy

a) The damaged and manufacturing defect related products shall be taken back by the Vendor at its cost. Payments, if any, made by the Company shall be refunded within days from the date of intimation of the defective/damaged products by the Company or the Company is entitled to adjust such amount at the time of periodical reconciliation.

b) The Vendor/Manufacturer shall be responsible for disputes/issues/claims raised by the End User/customer that are attributable to any manufacturing defects/damage relating to the products supplied by the Vendor/Manufacturer.

7. Termination

a) Either party shall have the liberty to terminate this TOT by giving 30(Thirty) days written notice with or without assigning any reason.

b) The Company shall have the right to terminate this TOT forthwith, in the event of any breach/violation of this TOT by the Vendor.

8. Anti Bribery & Anti Corruption

a) The Vendor agrees that it has not and undertakes that it shall not engage in any conduct that may be in violation of the applicable anti-corruption and/or anti-bribery laws. The Vendor warrants and undertakes to the Company that the Vendor (i) will not offer gift, consideration or benefit of any kind, which constitute illegal or corrupt practice to anyone, either directly or indirectly, as an inducement or reward for execution of this TOT (ii) will ensure that any person who performs or has performed services for or on its behalf in connection with this TOT complies with this Clause. (iii) will not enter into any agreement/arrangements with any person in connection with this TOT, unless such agreement/arrangements contain undertakings on the same terms as contained in this Clause. (iv) has and will maintain in place effective accounting procedures and internal controls necessary to record all expenditure in connection with the TOT. (v) from time to time, at the reasonable request of the Company, will confirm in writing that it has complied with its undertakings in this Clause and will provide any information reasonably requested by the Company in support of such compliance. (vi) The Vendor shall notify the Company as soon as practicable of any breach of any of the undertakings contained within this Clause of which it becomes aware.

b) In the event, the company detects any such breaches by Vendor or any of its staff or any person on behalf of the Vendor, the same will constitute a fundamental and material breach of this TOT following which the Company shall be entitled to terminate the TOT and the Vendor shall be liable to pay the Company double the value of purchase made by the Company for the last 6 months. The Vendor shall hold the Company harmless for all losses and expenses arising out of such violation. Any amount payable by the Vendor due to this breach shall be adjusted/deducted from any amount payable in terms of any purchase Order.

9. Confidentiality

The Vendor shall treat as confidential all information provided by the Company to the Vendor, including without limitation financial transactions. All confidential information provided by the Company hereto shall be used by the Vendor hereto solely for the purposes of this TOT. Except as may be required in carrying out the terms of this TOT, confidential information shall not be disclosed to any third party without the prior consent of the Company.

10. Data Privacy

a) In connection with this business transaction, the Vendor acknowledges and agrees that in the course of the business, it shall not process personal data and nor permit its Sub-contractors/employees to process personal data of the Company/ its employees for any other purpose other than performing its obligation under this TOT. The Vendor shall be liable for the acts or omissions of its Sub-contractors/employees in connection with the processing of personal data and shall intimate the Company about any data breach within 24 hours. The Vendor shall and cause its Sub-contractors/employees to comply with any direction the Company may issue on the processing personal data for compliance with applicable Laws. The Vendor shall process data strictly in accordance with "Data Protection Laws" as per the Digital Personal Data Protection Act of 2023 and Rules made thereunder, as may be amended from time to time. On Company's request or upon termination of this TOT, the Vendor shall instruct its Sub-Contractors/employees to delete the personal data received by them.

b) The Vendor hereby explicitly and unambiguously consents to the collection, use and transfer, in electronic or other form, of the Vendor's/its employees' personal data, Phone Number, Email Id and any other materials as applicable. The Company shall process such Personal Data in the capacity of a Data Fiduciary in accordance with Company's Privacy Notice (Annexure A). The Company will collect and process information for the following business purposes:

(i) As required to do so by local applicable law.
(ii) Order Processing: To fulfil the orders, process payments, and related services.
(iii) Support: To provide support and address your inquiries or concerns.
(iv) Marketing and Communication: To send promotional offers, newsletters, and updates about our products and services.
(v) Analytics: To improve our website and services by analysing user behaviour and preferences.

c) Sharing Your Information — Service Providers who help process payments and fulfil orders; and disclosures required by law or to protect rights and safety.

11. Audit

The Vendor agrees that all accounts, books and other records pertaining to the transactions under this TOT will be subject to reasonable periodic examinations and audit by the Company or by any third-party auditor or adviser.

12. Indemnity

The Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Company against any claim, loss, litigation cost or damage suffered by the Company on account of any action taken by any governmental authority, third party claim arising out of or in connection with i) design, manufacture, packaging, labelling, distribution, marketing, Standards, quality, use or sale of the product, ii) in respect of non-compliance omissions or delay in any of the terms and conditions of this TOT or observance of any statutory requirement or legal dues of any nature. iii) any act or omission by the employees/personnel of the vendor, in performance of their duties.

13. Amendments

No modification or amendment to this TOT and no waiver of any of the terms or conditions hereof shall be valid or binding unless made in writing and signed by both the parties.

14. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

This TOT shall be governed by the Laws in India and the courts of Ernakulam district will have exclusive jurisdiction to adjudicate any dispute arising under or in connection with this TOT.
